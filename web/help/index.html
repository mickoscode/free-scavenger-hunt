For players

You do not need an account or an app. A hunt is played in your phone's web browser, as a team.

Joining a hunt

  1. Scan the QR code your organiser gave you with your phone's camera, and open the link it shows. Or open the site's front page and type the hunt's address in the Joining a hunt? box, like ab3k/beach-hunt.
  2. Choose a team name, and press Join. If the organiser turned team names off you are simply given a number, like Team 7.
  3. If the hunt asks for a join code, type it in. Your organiser will tell you what it is, usually in person.

You can join before the game starts: the page shows a countdown. One team uses one phone. Your phone remembers your team, so you can close the browser, switch apps or lock the screen and come back. If you land on the front page by accident, it shows a Return to your game button.

Playing a hunt

  • The top of the page shows Time left on the left and your team's Score on the right. Open Instructions any time to read the rules.
  • The game is split into zones, usually areas of the venue. Tap a zone to see its map (tap the map to enlarge it) and its questions. The ‹ All zones button takes you back to the list, and ‹ Scoreboard shows how everyone is doing.
  • You can answer each question once, and the first answer counts, so check it before you press Submit. The question then shows Done. You will not be told whether it was right until the final results are published.
  • Text answers can have small typing mistakes. Number answers take digits only.
  • Photo challenges ask for a photo. Take it, send it to the organiser's WhatsApp group (the page has an Open WhatsApp group button), then press Photo sent to WhatsApp. The organiser scores it later. Photo challenges are optional and not every hunt has them.
  • Questions are only available while the game is on. When the time runs out, no more answers can be sent.

Provisional and final scores

While a game is running, and until the organiser says otherwise, the scoreboard heading reads provisional results. Your score goes up at once when a question you answered is marked right. Photo challenges are different: the organiser looks at each photo afterwards and gives full, half or zero points, so those points arrive later and your place can change.

Teams on the same score share a place (1, 1, 3). After the game, once the organiser has scored every photo, they publish the final results. The heading changes to final results, a Results button appears, and each team can see every question with its own answer, the official answer, whether it was right and the points. Tap a team's name on the scoreboard to see a summary of how it did, without the answers.

For organisers

Everything you need is on the site: building the questions, uploading maps, settings, sharing and scoring. There are no files to prepare elsewhere and no programs to install.

Getting started

  1. Choose Create your hunt on the front page, then Create an organiser account: a username and a password of at least 10 characters. There is no email address.
  2. You are shown recovery codes, once. Save them in a password manager or print them. If you forget your password, they are the only way back in without asking anyone. See Your account.
  3. Your account comes with a Demo Hunt. It opens 5 minutes after you register and runs for 15 minutes: open it on your phone, join as a team and play it, to see what your players will see.
  4. My hunts lists your hunts. Each one has four buttons: Manage › (or click the card) opens it, Play › opens the page players see in a new tab, Clone › makes a copy, and Data › opens the export and import page.

Creating a hunt

There are three ways. Use whichever suits you; they all end in the same place, an ordinary hunt you can edit on the Questions tab.

1. The form: start from nothing

On My hunts, Create a new hunt: give it a name, a web address and a time zone. The address fills in from the name; it is the last part of the link players use (/e/<your code>/beach-hunt/), it uses letters, numbers and dashes, and it cannot be changed later. Then add zones and questions on the Questions tab.

2. Clone: the quick way to start

Press Clone › on any hunt, including your Demo Hunt or last year's event. A small form appears with a new name and address already filled in (v2-…); change them if you like and press Create clone. The copy has the original's settings, instructions, zones, questions and maps. It does not copy teams or scores, and its day starts as today, so set the date on the Settings tab.

3. Export and import: the fastest for big hunts

If you are comfortable editing text, this is quickest. On a hunt's Data tab, Download hunt file saves one plain-text .json file with the settings, instructions, zones, questions and answers, plus one picture file per zone map. Edit the text file in any text editor (or paste it into an AI chat and ask for more questions in the same format), then open the Data tab of the hunt you want to fill, choose the .json file and its pictures together, press Check the file and then Replace this hunt with this file. The site checks the file first and says in plain words what to fix; nothing changes if it finds a problem. Pictures are matched by file name ("map": "./zone1-map.png" uses the file zone1-map.png).

Import replaces the hunt's settings, instructions, zones, questions and maps, so use a new or empty hunt, and it is locked once teams have joined. The file contains the answers: keep it private. It does not contain teams, scores, the join code or the WhatsApp link.

The phases of a hunt

A hunt is always in one of four phases, shown as a badge at the top of its page. The site's clock decides which, never the players' phones.

BadgeMeansWhat happens
Setting upNo questions yet, or settings incomplete.Build it. Nobody can join yet.
Waiting to startReady, before the start time.Players can join and see a countdown. You can still change anything.
In progressBetween the start and end times.Players answer questions. Settings are locked (see Settings); questions are locked once a team has joined.
FinishedAfter the end time.No more joining or answers. You score photos, then publish the final results.

Scores have their own two states. They are provisional from the start, and become final when you press Publish final results on the Scoring tab. You can only publish after the game has finished, and you can go back to provisional later to correct a score. Changing the settings, or deleting player data, also takes a hunt back to provisional.

Managing a hunt: the tabs

Press Manage › on My hunts. A hunt has six tabs.

Overview

The Share this hunt panel (see Sharing), a checklist of what is ready (questions, when, teams joined) with links to fix what is missing, and the Danger zone: Delete all player data (type delete; removes every team and score and keeps the questions) and Delete this hunt (type its address).

Settings

The hunt's name, time zone, day, start and end times, whether teams choose names, an optional join code, and your WhatsApp group link (with a Test link button to try it). The end must be after the start; if you set one so it is not, the other moves by 10 minutes to keep them in order. A game cannot run past midnight. Press the small ? beside a heading for an explanation. While the game is in progress these are locked; to change one anyway, type override in the box that appears.

Questions

The editor for the hunt itself.

  • Zones are areas, each with a name and an optional map image (any picture; the site shrinks it for phones, and players can tap it to enlarge). Zones can be moved, copied and removed. With several zones, press a zone's header to fold it away, or use Fold all zones and Open all zones, to keep a long page easy to move around.
  • Questions come in four kinds: Text answer (list every accepted answer, one per line; small typing mistakes are allowed), Whole number, Decimal number (a number like 2.5) and Photo challenge (see Photo challenges). Each has points. Move, copy or remove them with the buttons on the question.
  • Instructions for players are shown on the play page. Use simple formatting: # heading, - bullet, **bold**, *italic*. A preview shows how it will look.
  • Press Save questions: the site tells you exactly which zone and question to fix if something is missing. Questions are locked once a team has joined; use Delete all player data on the Overview tab to change them (do this after a rehearsal).

Teams

The live scoreboard, as players see it. Tap a team to see its answers exactly as it sees them, or to remove a team (for example a duplicate or an unsuitable name).

Scoring

The queue of photo challenges teams have said they sent, in the order sent. Check each photo in your WhatsApp group, choose full, half or zero and press Apply; applying again corrects an earlier choice. Photos never scored earn zero. When you have scored everything and the game has finished, tick I have applied all scores and press Publish final results. Teams then see their full results. While results are published scoring is read-only; Return to provisional unlocks it.

Data

Export this hunt to a file, or import a file into it, as described in Creating a hunt. Export always works; import is locked once teams have joined.

Sharing: QR codes, join codes and team names

Sharing the hunt

On the Overview tab, press Share this hunt (the heading, with the share icon) or the QR code itself. One window opens with:

  • the hunt's link, with Copy link and, on phones, Share… to send it with your phone's own share sheet (messages, WhatsApp, email);
  • a large QR code that players scan with their phone camera, with Download image (a picture you can put in an email, a slide or a poster) and Print (a sheet showing the hunt's name, Scan to join, the code and the address).

Put the QR code where players will be before the game: on the screen at the start, or printed. Try it yourself with your own phone first.

Join code (optional)

A join code stops people who have only the link from joining. Set one on the Settings tab: 5 to 12 letters or digits (capitals, spaces and dashes are ignored). Players are asked for it when they join, so tell them in person or on the day's instructions rather than printing it on the QR sheet. The public only learns whether a code is needed, never what it is. Leave it empty and anyone with the link can join.

Team names (optional)

By default each team types a name (1 to 30 characters, different from other teams, with a basic filter for rude words). If you turn Teams choose a name off on the Settings tab, there is nothing to type: teams are simply Team 1, Team 2 and so on, which suits groups of children or events where you would rather not display chosen names. Either way each team gets a number, which is how you identify it in the Scoring tab and in WhatsApp photos.

WhatsApp group

If you use photo challenges, paste your WhatsApp group's invite link on the Settings tab. It is shown only to teams who have joined, never to the public. Use Test link to check it opens.

Photo challenges

Optional, and many good hunts have none. A photo challenge asks teams to photograph something. Photos never go to this site: teams send them to a WhatsApp group that you run, then press Photo sent to WhatsApp so the site can list it for you. You look at the photo in WhatsApp and score it on the Scoring tab.

You are responsible for your group: only invite people who are part of your event, tell them what the photos are for, and follow the child-safety and privacy rules of your own organisation. Please read the photo challenges section of the About page before you use them.

On the day, and afterwards

  1. Rehearse. Open your hunt on a phone, join, answer a few questions, press Play › to see it as a player does. Then, on the Overview tab, Delete all player data so the real game starts clean.
  2. Check the settings: day, start and end times and time zone. Share the QR code.
  3. During the game watch the Teams tab and score photos as they arrive, so the provisional scoreboard stays close to the truth.
  4. After the end, finish scoring, tick I have applied all scores and Publish final results. Tell teams to press Results.
  5. Next time, Clone › the hunt and change the date. When you are finished with a hunt, Delete this hunt removes it and everything in it.

Your account

  • Recovery codes: if you forget your password, choose Forgot your password? on the sign in page, then enter your username, one recovery code and a new password. Each code works once. You can make a new set any time from My hunts (the old set stops working); do that if you are running low. Keep them somewhere safe: anyone with a code and your username can take over your account.
  • Change password and Delete my account are at the bottom of My hunts. Deleting your account removes all your hunts, with their teams and scores, and cannot be undone.
  • Privacy: the site stores no email address, real name or phone number. See the About page.

Limits

The site is free, with sensible limits so nobody can overload it for everyone else: up to 20 zones per hunt and 30 questions per zone, 300 teams per hunt, a limited number of hunts per organiser (the My hunts page tells you when you reach it; delete one to make room), and maps of up to 500 KB each after the site shrinks them.

Still stuck? Try the Demo Hunt, it shows most of this in action.
