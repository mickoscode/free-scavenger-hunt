For players

Joining a hunt

  1. Scan the QR code and open the link. Or type the hunt's address in the Joining a hunt? box on the front page.
  2. Choose a team name (or you get a number, like Team 7) and enter the join code if asked.

You can join before the game starts: the page says when it begins, and counts down on the day. One team uses one phone, and it remembers you: close the browser and come back any time. If you end up on the front page, press Return to your game.

Playing a hunt

  • The top shows Time left and your Score. Instructions has the rules.
  • Tap a zone to see its map and questions.
  • Each question can be answered once, so check before you Submit. You will not be told if it was right until the final results.
  • Photo challenges: take the photo, send it to the organiser's WhatsApp group, then press Photo sent to WhatsApp.
  • No more answers once the time is up.

Provisional and final scores

Until the organiser publishes the results, the scoreboard says provisional results. Right answers count at once; photo challenges are scored by the organiser later, so places can change.

After the game the organiser publishes the final results. The organiser can also give a team one bonus or penalty, such as fair play or returning late. It is added at this point and shown on your results page with the reason. Then tap Results to see every answer, the official answers and your points. Teams on the same score share a place (1, 1, 3).

For organisers

Getting started

  • Create an account: a username and a password of 10+ characters. No email.
  • Save your recovery codes when shown. They are the only way back in if you forget your password (see Your account).
  • Try the Demo Hunt. It opens 5 minutes after you register and runs for 15, so you can play it as a player.
  • My hunts lists your hunts. Each has Manage › (or click the card), Play › (the page players see), Clone › and Data ›.

Creating a hunt

Three ways. All give an ordinary hunt you can edit on the Questions tab.

  • The form. Create a new hunt on My hunts: a name, a web address and a time zone. The address is the end of the players' link and cannot be changed later.
  • Clone ›. Copies a hunt's settings, instructions, zones, questions and maps (not teams or scores). Pick a new name and address, then set the date. Best for the Demo Hunt or next year's event.
  • Export and import. For big hunts, if you are happy editing text. On the Data tab, Download hunt file saves one .json file plus a picture per zone map. Edit it (or have an AI chat write more questions in the same format), then on the target hunt's Data tab choose the file and pictures together and Check the file. Mistakes are reported before anything changes. Import replaces the hunt's content and is locked once teams join. The file contains the answers, so keep it private; it has no teams, join code or WhatsApp link.

The phases of a hunt

The badge at the top of a hunt shows its phase. The site's clock decides, not the players' phones.

BadgeWhat happens
Setting upNo questions yet. Nobody can join.
Waiting to startPlayers can join, and see when the game starts. Everything is editable.
In progressPlayers answer. Settings are locked (type override to change one); questions are locked once a team joins.
FinishedNo more answers. Score photos, add adjustments, then publish.

Scores are provisional until you press Publish final results (only after the game has finished). You can return to provisional to make corrections. Changing settings or deleting player data also returns to provisional.

Managing a hunt: the tabs

Press Manage ›. Press a ? beside a heading for a short explanation.

Overview

Share the hunt (see Sharing), check the checklist, and use the Danger zone: Delete all player data (type delete; keeps the questions) or Delete this hunt.

Settings

Name, time zone, day, start and end times, team names on or off, join code and WhatsApp link (with a Test link button). The end must be after the start; if not, the other time moves by 10 minutes. Games cannot pass midnight.

Questions

  • Zones are areas, each with a name and an optional map image. Move, copy or remove them. With several zones, press a zone's header to fold it.
  • Questions: Text answer (one accepted answer per line; small typos are allowed), Whole number, Decimal number or Photo challenge, each with points.
  • Instructions for players use simple formatting (# heading, - bullet, **bold**), with a preview.
  • Press Save questions. They lock once a team joins: after a rehearsal use Delete all player data.

Teams

The live scoreboard. Tap a team to see its answers as it sees them, or to remove it.

Scoring

Three parts, from the top:

  • Final results: when everything is scored, tick I have applied all scores and press Publish final results. Teams then see their full results. Return to provisional unlocks scoring again.
  • Photo challenges: check each photo in WhatsApp, choose full, half or zero and press Apply. Unscored photos earn zero.
  • Score adjustments (optional): one bonus or penalty per team.
Score adjustments

For things the questions do not cover, like Bonus for fair play or Penalty for returning late. Find the team, type the points (a minus for a penalty) and a reason, and it saves by itself. The row shows the team's final total. Clear the points to remove it.

  • A team has one adjustment, up to 1000 points either way. A new one replaces the old.
  • It counts only when you publish the final results: until then the scoreboard ignores it. After publishing, it is part of every total and place.
  • The team sees the points and your reason on its results page. Anyone viewing the team's public summary sees the points only.
  • Reasons you have used are suggested for the next team. With more than six teams, use Find a team.

Data

Export or import the hunt as a file (see Creating a hunt). Export always works; import is locked once teams join.

Sharing: QR codes, join codes and team names

  • Share this hunt (Overview tab; the heading or the QR code) opens the link with Copy link and, on phones, Share…, and a large QR code with Download image and Print (a sheet with the hunt's name and Scan to join). Test it with your own phone first.
  • Join code (optional): 5 to 12 letters or digits, set on the Settings tab. Tell players in person rather than printing it on the sheet. Without one, anyone with the link can join.
  • Team names: teams type a name by default. Turn Teams choose a name off and they are simply Team 1, Team 2... Either way each team has a number, used on the Scoring tab and in photos.
  • WhatsApp group: paste its invite link on the Settings tab. Only teams that have joined see it.

Photo challenges

Optional. Photos never go to this site: teams send them to a WhatsApp group you run, then press Photo sent to WhatsApp so you can score them on the Scoring tab. You are responsible for your group: invite only people in your event and follow your organisation's child-safety rules. Read the About page first.

On the day, and afterwards

  1. Rehearse: join as a team on a phone, answer a few questions, then Delete all player data on the Overview tab.
  2. Check the day, times and time zone, and share the QR code.
  3. During the game watch the Teams tab and score photos as they arrive.
  4. After the end: finish scoring, add any adjustments, tick I have applied all scores and Publish final results. Tell teams to press Results.
  5. Next time: Clone › the hunt and change the date.

Your account

  • Forgot your password? On the sign in page enter your username, one recovery code and a new password. Each code works once. Make a new set on My hunts when running low (the old set stops working). Keep them safe: a code plus your username gives access to your account.
  • Change password and Delete my account are at the bottom of My hunts. Deleting removes all your hunts.
  • The site stores no email, real name or phone number: see About.

Limits

Up to 20 zones per hunt, 30 questions per zone, 300 teams per hunt, maps up to 500 KB each after shrinking, and a limited number of hunts per organiser (My hunts tells you when you reach it).
