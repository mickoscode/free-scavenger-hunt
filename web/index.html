Free Scavenger Hunt

Run a scavenger hunt for your school, club or community. Teams join on their phones, answer questions around your venue and race the clock. No apps, no cost.

Scan the QR code your organiser gave you, or type the hunt's address below (for example ab3k/beach-hunt).

/e/

How it works

  1. Organisers create an event: zones with maps, questions (text, numbers or photo challenges), a start and finish time.
  2. Teams join by QR code, pick a team name, and answer in each zone while the clock runs.
  3. Everyone watches a live scoreboard. The organiser scores photos, then publishes the final results with a full breakdown for each team.
