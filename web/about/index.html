🌞 Why this site exists

I grew up in the 80s, when a good day meant being outside with your mates, running around and making up games. I never really grew out of it. I love games, and I love running around.

This site is an attempt to bottle a bit of that feeling: get people off the couch, out in the sun, working together and having a laugh, with a phone in the pocket only to keep score.

🌊 The surf club

I'm a proud volunteer and member of Sorrento Surf Life Saving Club. As a senior age manager I created several scavenger hunts for our youth cohort. They were great fun, but they took a lot of work: writing all the questions, then printing and handing out the games. All that printing also limited how many players could take part.

I wanted software to do the heavy lifting, so that anyone running a club, school, scout group or street party could build a hunt in an afternoon and have as many teams as they like.

This is my own project. It isn't run by, or officially connected to, the club.

🏃 The parkrun effect

I enjoy running, and the whole idea of parkrun is a huge inspiration: free, friendly community events, made possible by volunteers, open to everybody. Nobody needs a membership or a budget to show up and have a good time.

I wanted scavenger hunts to work the same way: free for the community, organised by volunteers.

This site isn't connected to parkrun. It's just where I found the inspiration.

🎁 Why it's free

Volunteers already give their time. The tools shouldn't add a bill. The site is built to be very cheap to run, and I'm happy to cover those small costs myself.

  • No ads.
  • No sign-up fee, no free trial, no "premium" version.
  • No limits designed to make you pay (there are sensible safety limits, so one person can't overload the site for everyone else).

🔒 Why it doesn't harvest your data

Because I don't want your data. A scavenger hunt only needs to know who is on which team and what they answered, so that is all the site knows.

What the site keeps

  • Organisers: a username, a scrambled copy of the password (and of the recovery codes), and the hunts they build. No email address, no real name, no phone number.
  • Players: the team name they type, their answers and their score, only for as long as the hunt exists. No accounts, no personal details.
  • A small cookie that keeps an organiser signed in, or a team in its game. Your phone also remembers the last hunt you played, only so it can offer to take you back.

What it never does

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no visitor logs.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or widgets. The site only loads its own files, and your browser enforces that.
  • No selling, sharing or handing over of data to anyone.
  • No collecting of email addresses: that is also why there is no "reset by email", and why organisers get recovery codes instead.

Organisers can delete a hunt, or their whole account, whenever they like, and everything goes with it.

📸 Photo challenges: optional, and never on this site

Photo challenges are completely optional. An organiser can run a great hunt with none at all (the Demo Hunt has none). If an organiser does want them, this is how it works, and why I built it this way.

  • The organiser creates and runs their own WhatsApp group for the event, and teams send their photos there.
  • Photos are never sent to, shared with or stored by this site. The site cannot even receive one. It only records that a team pressed Photo sent to WhatsApp, and the organiser then scores that challenge here.
  • The group's invite link is only shown to teams who have joined that hunt, never to the public.

I never want to hold anyone's photos, especially children's, so the site is built in a way that it simply can't.

⚠️ Please read before joining a photo group

  • Only take part if you know and trust the organiser.
  • Never join a WhatsApp group, or share a photo, unless you are 100% sure who runs it and you trust them completely. If you have any doubt, don't. You can skip the photo challenges and still play the rest of the hunt.
  • Children should only join a photo group with the knowledge and agreement of a parent or guardian.
  • Organisers: you are responsible for your group. Only invite people who are part of your event, tell players what the photos are for, think about what group members can see about each other, delete the group when the event is over, and follow the child-safety and privacy rules of your own organisation.

WhatsApp is a separate service run by someone else, with its own rules and privacy policy. This site doesn't run, monitor, moderate or verify any WhatsApp group, and has no way of seeing what is shared in one. This site accepts no responsibility for any group, any photo, or anything that happens between organisers, players or anyone else because of them. Everyone takes part, and runs hunts, at their own risk and judgement.

🔍 Open for anyone to check

Have fun out there, and thank you to every volunteer who makes community events happen.
