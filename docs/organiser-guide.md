# Organiser guide

1. **Create an account** at `/admin/` (a username and a password of 10+ characters; there is no email). You are then shown **8 recovery codes, once**: save them in a password manager or print them. If you forget your password, choose *Forgot your password?* on the sign in page and enter your username, one code and a new password. Each code works once; make new ones from your dashboard any time.
2. **Try the Demo Hunt first**: your account comes with one. It opens 5 minutes after you register and runs for 15 minutes. Open it on your phone, join as a team, and play: you will see the countdown, the zones, the questions, and the scoreboard. When you are ready, press **Clone ›** next to it under **My hunts** to make your own copy, which is quicker than starting from nothing.
3. **Or create a hunt from scratch**: a name, an address (the name in the web address, after your organiser code, e.g. `/e/bgh1/beach-hunt/`) and your time zone.
4. **Questions tab**: add zones (areas of your venue). Give each a name and, if you like, a map image. Add questions (text, whole number, decimal) with the answer and points, and photo challenges (teams send a photo to your WhatsApp group). Write instructions for players. Save.
5. **Settings tab**: the day, start and end times, whether teams choose names, an optional join code to tell players in person, and your WhatsApp group's invite link. Save.
6. **Overview tab**: share the QR code or link. Do a rehearsal: open it on a phone, join, answer, then **Delete all player data** on the Overview tab (type `delete`) before the real game.
7. **During the game**: the **Teams** tab shows the live scoreboard and each team's answers; the **Scoring** tab lists photo submissions: check each in WhatsApp (team number, zone and challenge must be clear), choose full, half or zero, Apply.
8. **After the game**: finish scoring (and add any **score adjustments**, below), tick "I have applied all scores" and **Publish final results**. Teams then see their full results. You can return to provisional to correct a score.

Tips: questions are locked once a team has joined; delete player data to change them. Settings are locked while the game is running. **Clone ›** (under My hunts) to start next year's event from this one.

## Export and import (the Data tab)

Advanced, and optional. **Data ›** on My hunts opens a hunt's Data tab. *Download hunt file* saves one text file (`.json`) with the settings, instructions, zones, questions and answers, plus a picture file for each zone map: keep them together in one folder. Edit the text file in any text editor (or paste it into an AI chat and ask for more questions in the same format), then open the Data tab of a hunt and choose the file and its pictures together under *Import a hunt file*. Importing replaces that hunt's questions, so use a new hunt or one without teams. The file contains the answers, so keep it private; it does not contain teams, scores, the join code or the WhatsApp link.

## Score adjustments (Scoring tab)

Optional: give a team **one** bonus or penalty with a reason, for things the questions do not cover (*Bonus for fair play*, *Penalty for returning late*). On the **Scoring** tab, under *Score adjustments*, find the team (there is a search box with many teams), type the points (a minus is a penalty) and a reason: it saves by itself, and the row shows the team's final total. Clear the points to remove it. Reasons you have already used are suggested for the next team.

An adjustment **does not count until you publish the final results**: the scoreboard and places ignore it until then, and the *Final results* box reminds you how many teams have one. Once published it is part of every total and place, and the team sees the points and your reason on its results page (anyone viewing the team's public summary sees the points, not the reason). While results are published adjustments are read-only; *Return to provisional* to change one.
